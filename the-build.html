Home / The build

Six to eight weeks.
Not a misprint.

From the day we break ground to your first swim. Here’s exactly what happens, and when.

Why we’re faster

Speed without shortcuts.

Many Las Vegas pool builds take three months or more. Ours average six to eight weeks because the same project manager, the same trusted trades and the same suppliers work every job — and we schedule them back to back before we ever dig.

  • One project manager — Grant — from dig day to plaster
  • Permits, HOA and inspections handled by us
  • Honest updates: if a permit or HOA runs long, you hear it first from us
01Day 1

Consult at your home

Adrian or Kevin (often both) visit your home to talk through type, size and features, take every measurement, and answer questions. Most consults take 30 minutes to an hour.

0248 hrs

3D design + itemized bid

Bobby turns the measurements into photoreal renderings of your yard — sunrise, sunset, furniture and lighting. Change anything and we re-render it.

03Wk −2

Contract, $1,000 deposit, permits

Your deposit is always $1,000 — never more. We create the permit plan, prepare your HOA packet, pull city or county permits and handle engineering. Most HOAs and counties take 1–2 weeks each.

04Wk 1

Dig day

Layout painted, excavator in, spoils hauled. The hole matches your 3D plan to the inch.

05Wk 1–2

Forms, steel & plumbing

Forms set, rebar tied and inspected, then every return, drain and spa line is laid out and pressure-tested.

06Wk 3

Shotcrete

The structural shell is sprayed and cured. Your pool now has its final shape.

07Wk 4–5

Tile, coping & plaster

Waterline tile from Aquabella and interior finish by Adams Pool Solutions. Electrical finished, equipment set.

08Wk 5–6

Decking

Deck forms, pour and finish. Gate, walls and final inspection scheduled.

09Wk 6–8

Fill, startup & first swim

We fill, balance the water and walk you through your equipment and app. Then it’s yours.

Then you swim.
