Our story

Fast, because we’re organized.

For more than two decades Steve Breck Pools has turned ordinary Las Vegas backyards into places families actually use. We build custom concrete pools and spas across the valley, and we’re known for finishing in six to eight weeks — far quicker than most — without cutting corners on steel, plumbing, tile or finish.

It works because it’s the same people every time: our own project manager, trades we’ve worked with for years, and suppliers who know our schedule.
