Easy to own

In-floor cleaning.

The A&A in-floor system hides pop-up cleaning heads in the floor and steps of your pool and spa. Every 60 seconds a new set sweeps debris toward the drains, so the whole pool is cleaned about every hour while the filter runs.

  • No robots or hoses
  • Heated water returned from the bottom for even temperature — up to 50% heating savings
  • Fewer dead spots, less algae, lower chemical use
  • Factory-designed for your pool, with a limited lifetime warranty
