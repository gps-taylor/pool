Built for Vegas rules

Designed to get approved.

Clark County limits new residential pools to 600 square feet of water surface. We design to it from the first sketch, so the pool you fall in love with is the one that gets permitted.

  • Permit plans and HOA packets prepared in-house
  • City and county inspections scheduled for you — Las Vegas, Henderson, Clark County
  • Equipment specified for desert heat and hard water
  • Shade, deck temperature and evaporation considered in every design
